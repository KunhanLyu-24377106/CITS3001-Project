\problemname{Bring home the Treasure}

After the treacherous maze she had to face, Talia finally finds the treasure: the lost O(1) graph algorithms and their proofs (embedded into stone slabs)! However, throughout her tough journeys,
all she had were the gear that would hopefully help get her home, and a random knapsack she found! Considering that there are too many algorithms and proofs to fit 
into her bag, what algorithms and their respective proofs should she actually bring home? Note that the name of the algorithms are \em {one word only (no spaces)} and that 
no two algorithm proofs share the same value.


\section*{Input}
The first line contains an integer $n$ ($1 \le n \le 100$), the number of algorithm proof papers in the treasure, and integer $w$ ($1 \le n \le 1000$), 
the max weight you can fit into your bag. The next $n$ lines contains the name of the algorithm $A$ ($1 \le |A_i| \le $10), 
the value of that algorithm based on how useful it would be to the world $v$ ($1 \le v_i \le 10^5$)
and the weight of the algorithmic proofs $p$ ($1 \le p_i \le $w).

\section*{Output}
Outputs an integer: the maximum value of the optimal set of proofs she should fit in her bag $m$ ($0 \le m \le $n), 
and a list of proofs sorted by alphabetical order $S$ in the format of $s_1, \dots, s_m$, that she would be able to put into her knapsack.
