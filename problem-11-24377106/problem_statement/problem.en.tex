\problemname{Talia and the Tollkeepers' Promise}

Talia has reached the island of \emph{numbertheorious}, searching for the lost
proof of an $O(1)$ graph algorithm. Her map marks the island's ancient landmarks
with numbers, but their names have faded away. To find the next clue, she must
visit every landmark and copy its inscription, then return to her camp to study
the completed record. Perhaps the inscriptions will lead her to the hidden
chest of ancient scrolls.

The island's tollkeepers make an unusual promise: \emph{pay for a road once,
and that road remembers you}. Whenever Talia uses a particular road for the
first time, she pays its toll. After that, she can use the same road in either
direction any number of times for free. All roads can be travelled in either
direction, including before their toll has been paid.

Talia starts at landmark $1$, where her camp is located. She may visit
landmarks and traverse roads repeatedly. Different roads between the same
two landmarks have separate tolls: paying for one does not pay for another.
Her only expense is these road tolls. Travel time, distance, and the number
of crossings do not affect the cost. Some roads have fallen into ruin, so
the usable roads on the map might not allow her to reach every landmark.

What is the minimum total toll Talia must pay to visit every landmark at
least once and return to landmark $1$? Landmark $1$ is already visited at
the start, and she has not paid for any roads before the journey begins.

\section*{Input}
The input describes one map. The first line contains two integers $n$ and
$m$ ($1 \leq n \leq 200\,000$, $0 \leq m \leq 300\,000$), the number of
landmarks and usable roads.

Each of the next $m$ lines contains three integers $u$, $v$, and $w$
($1 \leq u,v \leq n$, $u \neq v$, $0 \leq w \leq 10^9$), describing
a road between landmarks $u$ and $v$ whose toll is $w$ coins.
Each line describes a distinct road. Multiple roads may join the same pair
of landmarks, tolls may be equal or zero, and roads are listed in no
particular order.

\section*{Output}
Output a single integer: the minimum total toll in coins. If the journey
is impossible, output \texttt{IMPOSSIBLE} instead.
If there is only one landmark, Talia can stay at camp and the answer is $0$.

\section*{Sample Explanations}
In Sample 1, Talia can follow
$1,2,3,2,4,5,4,2,1$. The first uses of the roads cost
$2+1+5+3=11$ coins. All later uses of those roads are free.
No valid journey costs less.

In Sample 2, she can use the zero-toll road between $1$ and $2$, then the
road from $2$ to $3$, and return along the same roads for a total of $4$.
The other road between $1$ and $2$ is a separate road and need not be used.

In Sample 3, landmarks $3$ and $4$ cannot be reached from camp. In Sample 4,
camp is the only landmark, so no roads are needed.
